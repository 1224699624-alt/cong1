# Narrative Report: YOLO+SAM Research Handoff

## Core Story

This file is the writing handoff for future ARIS paper-writing or research-summary workflows in the `YOLO+SAM` project.

The core objective is to improve bone-age related anatomical segmentation, especially epiphysis segmentation, by building on the current YOLO-guided SAM pipeline and its refiner family. The main research tension is between improving fine boundary structure and avoiding under-segmentation of thin epiphysis regions or over-smoothing bone gaps.
